# Print-kit organic appearance reference

The organic root blend and the short taper to a flat tip landing are a
**look**, not an aerodynamic section. Freeze that language:

| Appearance | Number | Role |
|------------|--------|------|
| Root scale | 1.28× sit-plane chord (was 1.38 on the 0021 snapshot) | Fatter first loft station on the plate top. Thicker 0024 needs less extra fat. |
| Root blend height | 6 mm exam floor (16 mm source) | Soft join, not a print cliff |
| Tip scale | 0.72× tip chord | Short taper into a planar landing |
| Tip taper height | 4 mm exam floor (12 mm source) | Last loft section stays flat |
| Through-plate stump | sit-plane chord | Print sit; not the aero station |

Snapshot of the kit that established this look (NACA 0021, c 56/42,
6 rollers) lives at:

`%USERPROFILE%\Documents\noBS-CAD\Print-Kit-Organic-Reference\`

Live aero numbers are in `scripts/fixtures/print-kit-tutor.spec.json`.
The section is **NACA 0024-4.5/3.5**. File map:
[PRINT_KIT_FILES.md](PRINT_KIT_FILES.md). Do not put whale tubercles or
metaball blobs on the blades — those are not the appearance reference
and they are not the 2026 low-TSR section.
